# OpenWhispr

Use voice for dictation, assistance and meeting notes. OpenWhispr connects these tasks to the speech and text resources you configure in Nimi.

## Get started

Open OpenWhispr from Nimi Home and keep Nimi running. Select the required AI resources, allow microphone access when prompted and use the App's recording controls to capture your speech.

Review the transcript before using or sharing it. Names, numbers and punctuation can be wrong. Voice assistance and meeting workflows may need additional text or audio capabilities beyond dictation.

## Privacy and conditions

Recording depends on operating-system permissions and the selected input device. Capture only conversations you are permitted to record. Nimi manages AI connections; Local processing uses the selected local resource, while Cloud processing sends the required recording or text to the selected service.

Saved work belongs to the App's workspace. Export important notes and recordings through the available controls before changing workspaces or removing data. A recording or request that failed is not a completed transcript.

For help, report the feature, selected input device and error to the App provider. Remove private recordings, transcripts and account details from reports.
